Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and a password (stored in hashed form). When you use Spendly, we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to authenticate you, record your expenses, and show you summaries and spending patterns. We do not sell your personal data or use your expense details for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We keep it for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal and expense data will be removed. No method of storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages are loaded. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want your data accessed or deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
